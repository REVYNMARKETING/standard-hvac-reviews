About Standard HVAC

Setting the Standard in Napa Valley HVAC

Since 2007, Standard HVAC has been Napa Valley's most trusted name for precision heating and cooling installation — heat pump installation in St. Helena, complete AC and furnace replacement in Napa, commercial HVAC for wineries and tasting rooms, and estate HVAC for custom homes from Calistoga to Carneros.

Homeowners, estate managers, and commercial property owners throughout Napa and Sonoma County choose Standard HVAC for clean workmanship, custom ducting built to fit specific spaces, and precision piping and wiring that reflects nearly two decades of specialized expertise.

Standard HVAC is part of the Standard family of companies — alongside Standard Service (maintenance and repair) and Standard Fab (custom sheet metal and welding) — covering every phase of HVAC for residential, estate, commercial, and industrial properties.
