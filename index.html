About Standard HVAC

Setting the Standard in Napa Valley HVAC

Since 2007, Standard HVAC has been Napa Valley's most trusted name for precision heating and cooling installation. Whether you need a full heat pump installation in St. Helena, a complete AC and furnace replacement in Napa, commercial HVAC for a Rutherford winery, or estate HVAC for a custom home in Calistoga — Standard HVAC delivers commercial-grade craftsmanship on every project.

Homeowners, estate managers, and commercial property owners throughout Napa and Sonoma County trust Standard HVAC for clean workmanship, custom ducting built for specific spaces, and precision piping and wiring that reflects 19 years of specialized expertise. From the Carneros District to Oakville, every installation carries the same commitment to excellence that founder Jeremy Jack built this company on.

Standard HVAC is part of the Standard family of companies — alongside Standard Service (HVAC maintenance and repair) and Standard Fab (custom sheet metal and welding) — covering every phase of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning for residential, estate, commercial, and industrial properties across the Napa Valley region.
